About PACE

PACE exists because engineering education should extend beyond textbooks.

We are the official student-led technical community under the Department of Computer and Electronics Engineering at Advanced College of Engineering and Management (ACEM), Kathmandu. Our mission is to create opportunities where innovators build ideas, curious learners explore new technologies, and teams solve real engineering problems — through hands-on workshops, project mentorship, competition preparation, and leadership experience that bridges classroom theory with practical impact.
