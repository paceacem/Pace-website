Why Join PACE?

Engineering education extends beyond textbooks. PACE gives you a space to build real projects, learn from peers, compete nationally, and grow as a leader — all within a supportive community at ACEM.

  • Hands-on workshops in software, electronics, and embedded systems
  • Project teams with mentorship and portfolio-building opportunities
  • Competition preparation for national and international tech festivals
  • Leadership roles that develop responsibility and initiative
  • Collaboration with faculty, alumni, and industry partners
  • A community of curious, disciplined, and creative engineers
