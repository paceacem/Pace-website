About PACE

PACE (Project Association for Computer and Electronics) is a student-led technical club under the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering at Advanced College of Engineering and Management (ACEM), Kathmandu.

Our mission is to provide hands-on learning opportunities by exploring practical implementation of topics beyond the regular curriculum through workshops, projects, and technical competitions. We nurture engineering skillsets, promote multi-disciplinary collaboration, and act as a springboard for student leadership and technical excellence.

What We Do

Technical Workshops

We organize technical workshops and hands-on sessions covering programming, embedded systems, and system design for ACEM students.

Technical Festivals

We build competitive project teams and represent our college in national and international tech festivals, including IIT Bombay Techfest.

Club Collaboration

We collaborate closely with other ACEM technical clubs (such as the Advanced Robotics Club — ARC) on integrated robotics and engineering challenges.
