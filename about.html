About PACE

Empowering engineering students through practical hands-on experience, collaborative project builds, and nationwide technical competitions.

Our Mission

PACE (Project Association for Computer and Electronics) is a student-led technical club under the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering at Advanced College of Engineering and Management (ACEM), Kathmandu.

Our mission is to provide hands-on learning opportunities by exploring practical implementation of topics beyond the regular curriculum through workshops, projects, and technical competitions. We bridge theoretical knowledge from textbooks with industry-relevant engineering skills in hardware prototyping, embedded systems, and software engineering.

Established Since 2018
Department Electronics & Computer Engineering (ECE)
Institution Advanced College of Engineering & Management (ACEM)
Location Kalanki-14, Kathmandu, Nepal

What We Do

Technical Workshops

Organizing hands-on sessions covering microcontrollers, embedded systems, PCB design, Git workflows, and web development for ACEM engineering students.

Technical Festivals

Forming dedicated project teams to participate and represent ACEM at national and international technology festivals, including IIT Bombay Techfest.

Club Collaboration

Collaborating with sister technical organizations such as the Advanced Robotics Club (ARC) on multidisciplinary engineering projects.

Membership Selection Process

Departmental Interview Selection

PACE membership is annual and exclusively open to students enrolled in the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering (ECE) at Advanced College of Engineering and Management (ACEM). Selection is conducted via interview-based evaluation by the executive panel. Note: There is no online application form on this website. Official notices for recruitment calls are announced through campus channels.
