About PACE

PACE (Project Association for Computer and Electronics) is a student-led technical club under the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering at Advanced College of Engineering and Management (ACEM), Kathmandu.

Our mission is to provide hands-on learning opportunities by exploring practical implementation of topics beyond the regular curriculum through workshops, projects, and technical competitions.

PACE Annual Membership Process

Membership selection is conducted annually for students of the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering (ECE) at ACEM through an interview-based selection process. There is no online application form — notices are published officially on campus prior to recruitment rounds.
